Frequently Asked Questions

How does the FreshPak Vacuum Sealer work?

The FreshPak Vacuum Sealer uses powerful vacuum technology to remove air from the sealing bag, preserving food's freshness and extending its shelf life.

Can I seal both dry and moist foods with FreshPak?

Yes, FreshPak offers multiple sealing modes, allowing you to seal both dry items like grains and moist foods like raw meat or seafood.

Are the vacuum sealing bags reusable?

FreshPak's vacuum sealing bags are designed for one-time use to ensure optimal freshness and safety. We recommend using new bags for each sealing session.

Is the FreshPak Vacuum Sealer easy to clean?

Absolutely! The sealing area can be easily wiped clean with a damp cloth. Make sure the unit is unplugged and cooled down before cleaning.
